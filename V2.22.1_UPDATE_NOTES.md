# Bot War Room V2.22.1 — $50 Training Without Starving Trades

V2.22 removed micro trades, but the soft risk-size percentage could still make the Council decide an otherwise approved setup did not "deserve" $50.

V2.22.1 fixes that.

## Rule now

If a setup has already cleared the Council / paper-training gate:
- it receives at least **$50**
- soft position-size warnings cannot shrink it to $8, $15 or $30
- qualified WATCH research probes also target at least $50

A trade can still wait or be rejected for real reasons:
- hard scam / security veto
- max open positions
- insufficient cash
- total portfolio exposure is full
- that chain's exposure cap is full

So the bots should continue getting meaningful trading reps instead of either micro-buying or sitting idle because a soft sizing calculation was below $50.

## Important
The $50 rule still does NOT apply to SELL / TP / EXIT orders. Guardian can always sell a small remainder or moonbag.

Live-money rules are unchanged.
