# Bot War Room V2.28 — Profit First

The entries were producing real paper upside, but too much of it was being handed back.

## Dedicated Exit Strategist

A separate background Exit Strategist now evaluates every open position on every Guardian cycle.

It is not an entry-voting Council seat. Its full-time job is capital management after entry.

### Profit ladder

- +25%: bank 20% of original position
- +50%: bank another 20%
- +100%: bank another 25%
- +200%: bank another 25%
- final 10% is the moonbag

This means a runner cannot reach +100% or +200% while the system still has 100% of the original position exposed.

### High-water protection

The Exit Strategist tracks how much of a runner's peak has been given back. Large winners get progressively protected instead of being allowed to round-trip.

### Dead trades

The Exit Strategist exits positions that stop progressing based on:

- time held
- current return
- time since last high
- Runner Exit Genome continuation score
- distribution risk
- buy/sell pressure

### Capital recycling

If paper cash falls below the $50 fresh-runner training floor, weak/stale positions become candidates for exit so the scanner can fund the next opportunity.

This does not reintroduce a kill switch. It does the opposite: it frees capital instead of blocking future trades.

## Persistent portfolio history

The portfolio chart no longer lives only in React/browser memory.

Equity history is now persisted in the paper-wallet Redis state and survives:

- browser refreshes
- Railway process restarts
- prior trading sessions
- paper bankroll resets

The server records:

- all-time high equity
- all-time low equity
- timestamps
- long-term equity history

The ALL chart now merges persistent server history with the current live mark.

The wallet chart also shows ALL-TIME HIGH and drawdown from that high.

Important: V2.28 can preserve history from deployment forward. Historical peaks that were never stored by older versions cannot be perfectly reconstructed after the fact.
