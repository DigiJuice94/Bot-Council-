# Bot War Room V2.22 — $50+ Meaningful Paper Training

The micro-buy behavior is removed from paper training.

## New training rule
By default:
- every NEW paper BUY must be at least **$50**
- every Guardian SCALE-IN / ADD must be at least **$50**
- if the Council/risk engine only supports a smaller allocation, the setup is **observed and filed, but not bought**
- WATCH research probes target about **6% of paper equity**, while still respecting the current risk capacity and cash available

The system does not blindly inflate a $15 risk-approved trade to $50. It first checks the current risk-capacity in dollars. If the setup cannot safely support the $50 minimum, Executor skips the trade and records why.

## Why this is better for training
A $1–$15 entry barely changes a $1,000 paper portfolio and creates noisy learning. $50+ entries make:
- entry quality matter
- exits matter
- position sizing matter
- realized P/L meaningful
- Runner/Dumper lessons easier to compare

## Important exit exception
The $50 floor applies to BUYs and scale-ins, **not sells**.

Guardian is still allowed to:
- take a partial profit under $50
- trim risk under $50
- close the remaining tail/moonbag under $50
- force an emergency/distressed exit

This prevents the minimum training size from trapping a position.

## Research bankroll
With no open positions, a paper wallet below the minimum training-buy floor is functionally unable to continue learning. The auto-refill threshold therefore defaults to the same $50 floor, while reset count and injected capital remain recorded.

## Optional Railway controls
- `PAPER_MIN_BUY_USD=50`
- `PAPER_MIN_SCALE_IN_USD=50`
- `PAPER_EXPLORATION_MAX_PCT=6`
- `PAPER_AUTO_REFILL_THRESHOLD_USD=50`

Live trading sizing is unchanged.
