# Bot War Room V2.14 — Runner Genome + Filing Cabinet

V2.14 changes the Council's primary mission from "find a trade" to **learn the repeatable differences between fresh meme-coin runners and dumpers, then trade those patterns while they are still early**.

## Main research loop

Every real candidate now becomes a persistent case file. The system stores early market-cap/liquidity/volume/holder/wallet-flow observations, Council decisions, Alpha/conviction, risk state and all eight bot summaries. Open cases are revisited over time so the system can label the actual outcome instead of learning only from coins it traded.

Labels include:
- runner: reaches the configured target (default $300K market cap)
- dumper: loses the configured amount from the first observation (default 60%)
- neutral: survives the research horizon without either extreme
- open: still being followed

The Filing Cabinet keeps the raw case history. Runner/dumper tells are derived from those case files and are marked Testing, Confirmed or Invalidated as evidence grows.

## All eight bots file knowledge

Launch Scout, Social Scout, Wallet Tracker, Quant, Contract, Bear, CIO and Executor each write their visible Council read into the case file. This does not expose hidden reasoning; it stores the same concise evidence/rationale already shown in the War Room.

## Runner Genome validation

V2.14 performs a simple chronological out-of-sample test using early features from already-labeled runner/dumper cases. The newest holdout cases are not used to build the centroids being tested. This is deliberately simple and auditable; it prevents the progress bar from treating hindsight-only observations as proven predictive knowledge.

## Paper trading remains active

Council BUYs and V2.13 WATCH exploration probes continue. Closed paper positions are attached to case files. A fresh-coin win counts toward the 100-win requirement only when:
- the token was entered within the configured fresh market-cap/age zone (default <= $50K MC and <= 24h old),
- the closed trade is net profitable,
- and the final return is at least the configured minimum (default +10%).

## Paper bankroll auto-refill

If the research paper wallet is exhausted and no positions are open, V2.14 automatically restarts it at `PAPER_STARTING_CASH_USD`. It does **not** erase fill history. Reset count and total injected research capital remain visible so a blown-up strategy cannot hide behind a reset.

## CODE DECIPHERING progress bar

The new Filing Cabinet panel has a persistent loading-style bar. It fills from validated progress across:
- case-file/labeled-sample maturity — 25%
- 100 successful fresh-coin wins — 25%
- chronological out-of-sample Runner Genome accuracy — 20%
- paper expectancy/profit factor — 15%
- drawdown stability — 10%
- provider coverage — 5%

The bar is capped at 99% until **every graduation requirement** passes. Only then does it show `CODE DECIPHERED` at 100%.

Default graduation requirements:
- 2,000 fresh launch case files
- 300 runner/dumper labeled cases
- 100 successful fresh-coin wins
- >= 65% out-of-sample Runner Genome accuracy
- >= 1.30 paper profit factor with enough closed trades
- positive paper expectancy
- <= 35% observed max adverse excursion
- >= 75% configured-provider coverage
- >= 3 confirmed runner/dumper tells

All thresholds are environment variables so they are explicit and auditable.

## Live unlock

`AUTO_ENABLE_LIVE_ON_CODE_DECIPHERED=true` makes the research gate eligible to auto-unlock at 100%. The main Railway dashboard process still does **not** hold or sign with private keys. When all gates pass, full Council BUYs can be forwarded to a dedicated `LIVE_EXECUTOR_URL` authenticated with `LIVE_EXECUTOR_TOKEN`, capped by `LIVE_MAX_NOTIONAL_USD`.

Without that external executor configured, the UI will show **LIVE READY — EXECUTOR REQUIRED** rather than pretending real-money execution is active.

## Existing V2.13 behavior retained

The provider waterfall, Moralis/Bitquery/Solana-RPC fallbacks, Jupiter -> 0x -> DEX-liquidity paper routing, decision funnel, shadow reviews, paper exploration, deterministic hard safety vetoes and Guardian position management remain part of the cumulative update.
