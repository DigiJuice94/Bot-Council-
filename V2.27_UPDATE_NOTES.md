# Bot War Room V2.27 — Local Independent Council

V2.26 coupled every autonomous scan to a paid OpenAI Responses API call. When the account returned HTTP 429 / no credits, the entire scan failed before `latestResult` could update. That froze the Live Feed card and spammed the Council chat with the same error.

V2.27 removes OpenAI/ChatGPT from the Council runtime completely.

## Council architecture

Seven specialist entities still work privately first:

- Early Runner Scout
- Narrative Ignition Scout
- Early Flow Analyst
- Runner Pattern Quant
- Fast Safety Gate
- Dumper Pattern Specialist
- Portfolio Strategist

Each specialist has:
- its own specialty scoring logic;
- its own Redis-backed private memory;
- no access to peer votes during the private round.

Only after all seven private opinions are locked does the meeting round run. Each entity may hold or change its vote while preserving its specialty.

Runner CIO remains a separate eighth entity and receives the completed meeting only after the seven specialists finish.

There are no external model/API calls and therefore no AI-credit bill.

## Reliability

- The autonomous loop no longer depends on OpenAI credits.
- Repeated infrastructure errors are de-duplicated so chat cannot be flooded every scan.
- Dashboard marks the Council as `8 INDEPENDENT LOCAL ENTITIES · $0 API`.
- The independent Council trace remains required.
- PAPER portfolio kill switches remain OFF.
- Hard token-level safety and real paper-wallet cash accounting remain.
