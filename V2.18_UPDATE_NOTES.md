# Bot War Room V2.18 — Paced Council Group Chat

The group chat now behaves like a real live conversation instead of dumping a whole Council meeting into the feed at once.

## New behavior
- new messages are queued and revealed **one at a time**
- default reveal pace is **1.15 seconds per message**
- scrolling upward automatically switches the group chat to **PAUSED**
- while paused, new messages are buffered instead of moving the feed
- the PAUSED chip shows how many new messages are waiting
- `Resume Live` jumps back to the bottom and restarts the one-at-a-time feed
- the live typing indicator is hidden while the chat is paused
- only a small six-message recent window is shown on initial load so the chat does not immediately create a huge scroll
- messages retain the original Council timestamp and chronological order
