# Bot War Room V2.17 — Dynamic Paper Position Sizing

The Council can now make materially larger PAPER positions while the Runner Genome is learning.

## New default paper sizing
- clean full Council BUY: up to **7.5% of current paper equity**
- 1 warning: capped around **5%**
- 2 warnings: capped around **3%**
- 3+ warnings: capped around **1.5%**
- missing ordinary holder/bundle/social/smart-money coverage: capped around **2.5%**
- missing critical sellability/honeypot/Solana-authority evidence: capped around **1%**
- liquidity under $50K: capped around **2%**
- qualifying WATCH research probe: up to **1.5%**
- total paper portfolio exposure: default cap **60%**
- one-chain paper exposure: default cap **30%**
- confirmed winners can scale toward a **15% gross position cap**

These are maximums, not forced position sizes. Regime sizing, Council conviction, liquidity/slippage, available cash, portfolio exposure and deterministic risk can still reduce or block the order.

## Winner scaling
The existing Guardian ADD1 / ADD2 / ADD3 confirmation system remains in place. It can now let a strong winner grow beyond the old 5% gross-position ceiling instead of being artificially cut off.

## Live trading
This update deliberately does **not** make eventual live trading equally aggressive. Live risk defaults remain at the previous conservative sizing unless separately configured later.

## Environment overrides
Optional Railway variables:
- `PAPER_MAX_POSITION_PCT=7.5`
- `PAPER_EXPLORATION_MAX_PCT=1.5`
- `PAPER_MAX_TOTAL_EXPOSURE_PCT=60`
- `PAPER_MAX_CHAIN_EXPOSURE_PCT=30`
- `PAPER_WINNER_MAX_GROSS_PCT=15`
- `LIVE_MAX_POSITION_PCT=2`

No variables are required; these values are the new defaults.
