# Bot War Room V2.28.1 — Reset Paper Wallet

Added a **RESET PAPER WALLET** button beside the paper wallet stats.

The reset:
- restores the configured paper starting bankroll (default $1,000);
- clears current open PAPER positions so old exposure does not inflate the fresh wallet;
- keeps closed trade history;
- keeps Runner Genome / Filing Cabinet data;
- keeps every entity's private memory;
- keeps all-time portfolio/equity history;
- adds a reset marker to the all-time chart;
- keeps fill/counter history for audit.

Open positions cleared by a manual reset are abandoned rather than turned into artificial wins/losses, so the reset does not poison strategy learning.

The button requires a confirmation click.
