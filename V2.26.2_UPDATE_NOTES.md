# Bot War Room V2.26.2 — PAPER Kill Switches Removed

## What was removed from PAPER Runner Lab

The paper research engine no longer stops or vetoes new opportunities because of:

- daily P/L or drawdown;
- maximum open-position count;
- total portfolio exposure percentage;
- per-chain exposure percentage.

A losing paper bankroll is research data, not a reason to stop the experiment.

## What still exists

These are not portfolio kill switches and remain intentionally active:

- explicit honeypot / sellability failures;
- mint/freeze/authority hard failures where verified;
- top-10 concentration above the hard 80% cap;
- bundled supply above the hard 25% cap;
- real paper-wallet cash availability;
- route / slippage feasibility.

The system cannot spend paper cash that does not exist, but it will no longer decide that a -5%, -10% or larger drawdown means the Council must stop taking fresh-runner reps.

## Independent Council runtime enforcement

The autonomous scanner now has a final post-patch guard:

- it must import and call `runIndependentCouncil`;
- it refuses a result that does not contain an `independentCouncil` trace;
- it cannot silently fall back to the legacy synthetic role engine.

The chat summary now explicitly says `PAPER kill switches OFF`.
